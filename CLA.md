# Contributor License Agreement

> **In plain English** — this is the summary, not the agreement. The binding text is
> below, and it's the standard, lawyer-drafted [Project
> Harmony](https://www.harmonyagreements.org) individual contributor agreement
> ("Option Five"), not something invented for this project.
>
> - **You keep the copyright to your work.** This is a license, not a copyright
>   assignment.
> - **You keep the right to use your own contribution however you like.**
> - **Your contribution stays open source.** It remains available to the public
>   under the MIT License, exactly as before.
> - **The project may also license it under other terms** — including copyleft,
>   commercial, or proprietary terms. This is what lets the project offer a
>   commercial edition, or change its license, in the future.
> - **You only sign once,** and it covers all your present and future
>   contributions to the project.
>
> The one concession you make is that a contribution can't be taken back later: the
> license is irrevocable, so that users of the project can rely on the code staying
> available.

## How to sign

Open (or update) a pull request on the repository. The CLA Assistant bot will
comment on it. Reply to that pull request with exactly:

```
I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA
```

Your reply is your electronic signature, and the bot records it together with your
GitHub username in `signatures/version1/cla.json` in this repository. You only need
to do this once; it covers all your future contributions. If the check ever looks
stale, reply `recheck`.

Read the agreement below before signing — it is legally binding.
