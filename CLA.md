## How to sign

Open (or update) a pull request on the repository. The CLA Assistant bot will
comment on it. Reply to that pull request with exactly:

```
I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA
```

The bot records your signature and the CLA check turns green. **You only sign
once** — it covers all your present and future contributions to the Project. If the
check ever looks stale, reply `recheck`.

Your signature, together with your GitHub username, is recorded in
`signatures/version1/cla.json` in this repository, so that the Project keeps a
durable record of the agreement.
